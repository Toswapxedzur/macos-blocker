# Signing helpers

- `vault-app-group.py`: validates and embeds authorized Mac app profiles, prepares a development wrapper, and verifies the resulting outer signature. Website release signing requires its production profile; development pairing remains optional.
- `test-vault-app-group.py`: mini1 metadata and launcher fixtures; no account credentials or live-signing claims.
- `README.md`: App Group signing, environment and verification contracts.
